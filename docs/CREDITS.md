# Credits

Third-party art used in Cooking With Fam. Everything else (UI, font, generated sprites,
pxl-drawn sprites) is original to this project.

## Free Ingredients Pack — caiomarcelpixel

Raw ingredient sprites (vegetables, fruit, meat, fish, dairy) in the `items` and `items64`
atlases. 64x64 pixel art.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-food-ingredients-pack
- License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: cropped to the sprite's bounds and scaled by whole numbers to fit our 32px and
  64px frames; no redrawing.
- The original zip is kept at `tools/raw/free_ingredients_pack01.zip` so the atlas build is
  reproducible.

Required attribution, also shown in the game's credits: "Ingredient art by caiomarcelpixel,
CC BY 4.0".

## Free Pixel foods — ghostpixxells

32x32 dishes and snacks (chips, cheese puffs, popcorn, cookies, burgers, ...) in the `items`
atlases. `tools/raw/packs/ghostpixxells_pixelfood_cc0.zip`.

- Source: https://ghostpixxells.itch.io/pixelfood
- License: CC0 1.0 (stated on the page: "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal")

## PixelFoodThemedAssets

Finished dishes and drinks in the `food` atlas (`tools/raw/PixelFoodThemedAssets.zip`).
Supplied by the project owner; the zip ships without a license file, so confirm its terms
with the author before any public release beyond the hackathon.
